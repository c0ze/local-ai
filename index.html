WebGPU is not available in this browser

This app runs AI models locally via WebGPU. Without it, model inference won't work.

  • Chrome / Edge 113+: enabled by default; otherwise your GPU may be blocklisted.
  • Safari 17.4+: requires macOS Sonoma.
  • Firefox: Nightly only, with dom.webgpu.enabled in about:config.
  • Linux: launch Chrome with --enable-features=Vulkan.
